###  Welcome to The Bellwether {.sticky-header}

"Bellwether" was Merriam-Webster's word of the day on September 27, 2026, the date I started making this template. I was looking for a perfect title, and I wasn't sure if I had found it, but it sounded *good enough* for me to stop my obsessive search. The meaning: "one that takes the lead or initiative; a leader" or "an indicator of trends." Though, to be honest, I'm not sure the meaning of the word is as important as the way the name came about -- a process that felt more open to chance than deliberation. I guess that means it was "meant to be"? If you can believe in such things.

*The Bellwether* is a two-tone 11ty starter kit for a content-focused blog. It is ideal for long-form writing, though it can be used for all forms of content. It is a bare-bones kit: it has a blog, tags page, and an about page. Individual pages for tags are auto-generated whenever a new tag is added to a post. With a simple design, adding additional pages (such as a blogroll, for example) is easy and will not break the layout.

### Who is it for? {.sticky-header}

This template was made with bloggers in mind. You may find The Bellwether to be a good choice if you:

- Write essay or long-form content
- Have a personal blog
- Write poetry or fiction
- Prefer static websites over large CMS platforms
- Appeciate minimalist design

On the other hand, it may not be the right choice for you if you prefer:

- Eye-popping visuals
- Extensive social features
- Heavy animations
- Complex panels, menus, and tooling
- A theme packed with integrations

Some excellent tools for those goals exist, but this project has a different focus.

### Design Principles {.sticky-header}

Here are a few guideposts that I have implemented in The Bellwether.

#### Content First

At the end of the day, a website is just housing what you put inside of it. Layouts and decorative elements should be complementary, not overwhelming.

#### Optimize for Immersion

"Immersive" web design invites readers in to stay a while. The Bellwether allows you to focus on long-form creative content that is substantive.

#### Simplicity Ages Well

Minimalism is the trend that has remained trendy through the decades. There's a reason it is perhaps the dominant philosophy in web design. In any website, the content should be easy to locate and follow.

#### Personalization Matters

Your website is uniquely *yours*. As such, a starter kit is just that: a starting point. This is a boilerplate. It's expected that you will add your own extras and flourishes and adaptation. The Bellwether has been designed to be easily customizable.

### About 11ty (and a confession) {.sticky-header}

[11ty](https://11ty.dev) (currently rebranding as Build Awesome) is a static site generator (SSG) that allows you full control over your site's output. I like 11ty because it doesn't lock you into a single templating language or data format; everything can be configured, but it works with zero configuration out of the box.

My confession is this: this is my *first* ever static site starter kit. I've built static sites before, mostly based upon *other* templates. [My blog](https://meganrenae.me) is a [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com) site that I built from the ground-up, with no starter.

11ty's flexibility, while overwhelming at first, was exactly what made it the perfect choice for this project. I could figure out how to get the look and feel *I* wanted, rather than to compromise based on what the underlying programming would allow. 

With that said, using a starter kit is the perfect place to begin for anyone wanting to learn the ins and outs of how 11ty works without feeling in over their head.
